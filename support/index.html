Questions about your atlas? Start here, or get in touch.

Marking a place

Open a country on the map and use its visit control to add or remove it from your atlas. Selected countries also have travel regions to explore. Countries and territories remain separate destinations.

Finding visits in Photos

Choose Find in Photos and allow access to the photos you want Inlay to read. Inlay uses the location saved with a photo to suggest places. Review the suggestions and choose which visits to add.

A photo without location information cannot suggest a place. Limited Photos access only includes the photos you selected. You can change Photos access in your device’s privacy settings.

Comparing with a friend

Open Friends and choose Invite a friend to share an atlas link. A friend’s atlas can show the places you share and the places only one of you has visited. Share links only with people you intend to receive your travel record.

If you enable Keep friends up to date, your chosen name and visited places are published through Apple’s CloudKit so the shared atlas can refresh. Turning this off stops future updates; it does not erase copies already received. See the privacy policy for details.

Artwork and offline use

Country artwork is included with Inlay. Regional artwork downloads separately as it becomes available. If regional detail has not downloaded, the country illustration remains available. Reconnect to the internet and return to the atlas to allow downloads to continue.

iCloud and permissions

Inlay can sync your travel record through your own iCloud account. Check that iCloud is enabled and that your devices use the same Apple Account. Photos and the optional one-time location request are controlled by your device’s privacy settings.

Get in touch

Email Inlay support, or open a support request on GitHub.

Include your device model, system version, Inlay version, and what happened. Please keep photos, private travel details, and friend invitation links out of public GitHub requests.
