Already have Inlay? Bring your places into the studio and make something personal.

Inlay is free on the App Store, and in your browser at inlayatlas.com/atlas.

  1. Begin in Inlay

    Open the print option for your atlas, a friend’s shared atlas, or a comparison. Check the people and places, then continue to the print shop.

  2. Make it personal

    Choose Your World, Country or Memory in the studio. Edit the names and dedication, or add photographs from your device.

  3. Keep your composition

    Download a PNG proof or save your design to return to later. Physical printing and ordering will follow.

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I use my actual Inlay map?

Yes. Open a print link from Inlay to bring your atlas into the studio. You can also open a saved design. The examples on these collection pages use sample travel history.

Can I use my own photographs?

Yes. Choose Memory in the studio, then select photographs from your device and add captions. Your photos stay in your browser and are included only in the files you choose to export.

Can I make something for a friend?

Use an atlas your friend has shared with you. For Our World, combine two atlases and keep the individual, shared and combined counts. It works for any two people.

Can I order a physical print?

For now, you can create and download a digital proof. Paper, physical sizes, frames, prices and delivery will be confirmed before ordering opens. The frames shown here are illustrative.

Is Inlay available on the App Store?

Yes. Inlay is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and you can use the same atlas in your browser at inlayatlas.com/atlas. Physical prints will be optional.
