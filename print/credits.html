About the artwork.

The illustrations and atlas compositions were created for Inlay. Each destination uses its own illustration and the same geographic outlines as the app.

Geography

Country and territory boundaries derive from Natural Earth and the world-atlas distribution. Pinned source references and the world-atlas license are included. Destination names and country/territory categories follow the Inlay catalog.

Flags

Flags by flag-icons, with MIT license. Every flag face is shown complete.

Lettering

Italiana, Instrument Serif and Instrument Sans are used under their included SIL Open Font Licenses.

Local link reading

Pako reads the app’s compressed print links on your device. MIT license.

Your proof

The studio prepares a personal digital proof. Physical print sizes, production, paper and fulfillment have not been selected. No payment or physical order is taken here. Photographs you choose are kept in this browser; read about your controls.
