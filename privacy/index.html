Inlay works without an Inlay account. Photos are analyzed on your device, and sharing is something you choose.

Privacy policy · Effective November 9, 2026
Developer: Jorge Aguirre

Your travel record

Inlay stores your visits and other atlas settings on your device. Supported travel data can sync through your own Apple iCloud account using Apple’s private key-value storage. The developer does not have access to that private iCloud storage.

Photos

With your permission, Inlay reads the locations and dates saved with accessible photos to suggest places you have visited, and displays your photos from those places. You choose which suggested visits to add. Inlay does not upload your photos to the developer or include them in its friend-sharing records.

Your Photos library and any iCloud Photos use remain governed by your Apple settings. You can limit or revoke Inlay’s Photos permission in system privacy settings.

One-time location

If you choose to mark where you are, Inlay requests your location to identify the destination on your device. Inlay does not continuously track you or store that precise coordinate. If you later share your atlas, the resulting visited destination may be included in the shared travel record.

Sharing with friends

A standard invitation link contains the name you choose and a snapshot of your visited places. Anyone you send the link to can read that snapshot. Copies received by another person are outside your control.

If you turn on Keep friends up to date, Inlay publishes your chosen name and visited destination identifiers to Apple’s CloudKit public database under a random sharing identifier. The identifier is included in your invitation link so friends can retrieve updates. The shared data does not contain your photos or precise photo coordinates. Inlay has no public browsing directory of shared atlases.

Turning live sharing off stops future updates. The last published record and copies already received by friends may remain. For questions or requests concerning a published record, email the developer; do not post your invitation link publicly.

Place information and artwork downloads

Place introductions include credited Wikipedia text. Displaying place photographs or requesting additional place information contacts Wikimedia services, which receive the requested resource and ordinary network information such as your IP address. Their privacy policy applies to those requests. Regional artwork is delivered through Apple’s Background Assets service.

Diagnostics, advertising and tracking

Inlay does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Performance reports generated by Apple’s MetricKit are saved on your device; Inlay does not upload them to the developer. Apple may separately handle diagnostics according to your system settings and Apple’s policies.

Your controls

You can change your visits, stop live updates, remove a saved friend, and manage Photos and Location permissions in system settings. Removing the app removes its local data according to the operating system’s behavior. iCloud storage, device backups, and copies of shared data may remain under their respective settings and recipients’ control.

Support and this website

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you include to respond to your request. Avoid sending private photos or travel details unless necessary. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics or advertising; GitHub handles ordinary hosting requests under its privacy statement.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as Inlay changes. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact Jorge Aguirre.
