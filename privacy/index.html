Inlay works without an account, and an account is yours to choose. Photos are analyzed on your device, and sharing is something you choose.

Privacy policy · Effective November 25, 2026
Developer: Jorge Aguirre

Your travel record

Inlay stores your visits and other atlas settings on your device. Supported travel data can sync through your own Apple iCloud account using Apple’s private key-value storage. The developer does not have access to that private iCloud storage.

The web atlas

The atlas at inlayatlas.com/atlas keeps your visits, plans, trips and settings in your browser’s own storage on your device. Without an account, they are not sent anywhere. Exporting creates a file you control; importing reads a file you choose, in the browser.

Optional account

You can sign in with Apple or Google, in the app or the web atlas, to keep one atlas in step across your devices. Without signing in, nothing below applies.

What is stored. Your travel record: visited places and their dates, Want to go plans, trips (names, dates, places and notes) and, from the app, the friends you have saved and the name and home country you choose. Each record carries the time it was last changed so your devices can agree on the newest version. Photos, photo locations and precise coordinates are never included. The sign-in itself stores an account identifier and the email address and name your provider shares; with Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address.

Where. The records are kept in Google Cloud Firestore, and sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, both operated by Google for Inlay; the data is stored in the United States and is encrypted in transit. The web atlas loads these Google services only when you sign in or are signed in.

Who can see it. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or change your records. The developer does not browse them and accesses them only when needed to run the service or to answer a request from you. They are not sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking.

Deleting it. Signing out stops syncing and lets you keep or remove the copy in that browser. Deleting the account, in Settings in the app or the web atlas, removes your synced records, friends list, profile and the account itself, and for Sign in with Apple revokes Inlay’s access. The copy on each of your devices stays unless you remove it. See Deleting your Inlay account, including what to do if you can no longer sign in.

Photos

With your permission, Inlay reads the locations and dates saved with accessible photos to suggest places you have visited, and displays your photos from those places. You choose which suggested visits to add. Inlay does not upload your photos to the developer or include them in its friend-sharing records.

Your Photos library and any iCloud Photos use remain governed by your Apple settings. You can limit or revoke Inlay’s Photos permission in system privacy settings.

One-time location

If you choose to mark where you are, Inlay requests your location to identify the destination on your device. Inlay does not continuously track you or store that precise coordinate. If you later share your atlas, the resulting visited destination may be included in the shared travel record.

Sharing with friends

A standard invitation link contains the name you choose and a snapshot of your visited places. Anyone you send the link to can read that snapshot. Copies received by another person are outside your control.

If you turn on Keep friends up to date, Inlay publishes your chosen name and visited destination identifiers to Apple’s CloudKit public database under a random sharing identifier. The identifier is included in your invitation link so friends can retrieve updates. The shared data does not contain your photos or precise photo coordinates. Inlay has no public browsing directory of shared atlases.

Turning live sharing off stops future updates. The last published record and copies already received by friends may remain. For questions or requests concerning a published record, email the developer; do not post your invitation link publicly.

Place information and artwork downloads

Place introductions include credited Wikipedia text. Displaying place photographs or requesting additional place information contacts Wikimedia services, which receive the requested resource and ordinary network information such as your IP address. Their privacy policy applies to those requests. Regional artwork is delivered through Apple’s Background Assets service.

Diagnostics, advertising and tracking

Inlay does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The optional account uses Google’s Firebase services only to sign you in and sync your atlas, not for analytics or advertising. Performance reports generated by Apple’s MetricKit are saved on your device; Inlay does not upload them to the developer. Apple may separately handle diagnostics according to your system settings and Apple’s policies.

Your controls

You can change your visits, stop live updates, remove a saved friend, sign out of or delete an optional account, and manage Photos and Location permissions in system settings. Removing the app removes its local data according to the operating system’s behavior. iCloud storage, device backups, and copies of shared data may remain under their respective settings and recipients’ control.

Support and this website

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you include to respond to your request. Avoid sending private photos or travel details unless necessary. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics or advertising; GitHub handles ordinary hosting requests under its privacy statement.

Personal print proofs

The Print studio reads the name and visited country and territory identifiers in an Inlay print link inside your browser. The link fragment is not sent as part of the page request. The studio requests public artwork and flag files for the proof from this website; those hosting requests can reveal which artwork is being loaded. Names and selected photographs are not automatically uploaded.

You choose any photographs for a Memory proof yourself. The studio keeps the draft and selected photographs in this browser on this device. Use Clear draft to remove that local draft. Downloading a proof or saving a design file creates a file you control; a saved design includes the atlas and any photographs you selected. A friend can use an atlas you have already shared with them to create a gift proof. Photos, trip dates, trips and plans are not included in that atlas handoff. This release does not take payments or place physical print orders.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as Inlay changes. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact Jorge Aguirre.
