Deleting your
Inlay account.

Accounts are optional. If you signed in with Apple or Google to sync your atlas, you can delete that account and everything Inlay keeps with it.

In the Inlay app

Open Settings, choose Sync across devices, then Delete account. Inlay asks you to confirm your sign-in once more, then deletes the account.

In the web atlas

Open your atlas in a browser, choose Settings, then Delete account under Sync across devices. Confirm, then sign in again with the same Apple or Google account when the window asks.

What is deleted

Your synced atlas (visited places, dates, Want to go plans and trips), your friends list, your profile and any live sharing record, then the account itself. For an account made with Sign in with Apple, Inlay also revokes its access to your Apple ID. Deletion happens right away and can’t be undone.

What stays

The copy of your atlas on each device or browser you used stays there, so you don’t lose your travels. You can remove it in the app or the browser, or by deleting the app. Copies of your atlas that friends have already received stay with them.

Can’t sign in?

If you can no longer reach the Apple or Google account you used, email the developer from the address linked to it and ask for your Inlay account to be deleted. You’ll get a reply once it’s done. Please don’t include travel details or invitation links.
