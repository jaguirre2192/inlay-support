Start with a print link from Inlay or a saved design, then choose Country. Digital proof only; ordering opens later.

How do I bring my atlas?

In Inlay, open the print option for your atlas, a friend or a comparison and continue to the print shop. The link brings the people and country lists into the studio. You can also paste that link in the studio.

What can I download?

A PNG proof of your composition and a saved design you can reopen. Physical sizes, materials and production will be confirmed before printed editions go on sale.
